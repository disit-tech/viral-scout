## Never (unattended)
Post to any social platform, schedule posts, spend money, buy ads, contact leads or customers, change a live website (unless the business rules above name the one allowed change), delete data, or change scheduled tasks. Delivering drafts to {{owner}} for review is fine. Writing a finished film or post into its queue with the status the job names (`✅ auto` when the owner turned on publish.approval: auto) is fine too: the posting job posts it, never you. If a step needs one of these, do not do it: write it in today's daily note and, if delivery is on, answer the note with `{{notes}} skip <id> "needs a live session: …"`.
Commit only files you created or changed for this job (`git add <paths>`, never `git add -A`). Never push unless the business rules above say so.
